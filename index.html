    Why protein matters on GLP-1

    Many people using GLP-1 medications eat less because appetite is reduced. Getting enough protein can help support muscle, recovery, and fullness during weight loss.

    Popular GLP-1 protein foods

    Chicken breast, eggs, salmon, tuna, Greek yogurt, tofu, cottage cheese, lean beef, protein shakes, and beans are common high-protein options.
