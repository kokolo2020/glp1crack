WEIGHT LOSS

How Much Weight Will You Lose on Ozempic or Wegovy? A Calculator Guide

One of the most common questions people ask before starting a GLP-1 medication is: how much weight can I actually expect to lose? The answer depends on which drug you take, your starting weight, and how long you stay on it.

Ozempic (semaglutide 0.5–2mg) was originally approved for type 2 diabetes but is widely used off-label for weight loss. In the SUSTAIN clinical trials, patients lost an average of 5–10% of body weight over 68 weeks. For a 90kg person, that's 4.5–9kg.

Wegovy (semaglutide 2.4mg) is the higher-dose version specifically approved for weight management. The STEP trials showed an average weight loss of 15–17% of body weight over 68 weeks — nearly double Ozempic's effect at the higher dose.

Mounjaro and Zepbound (tirzepatide) are dual GIP/GLP-1 agonists and the most effective option currently available. SURMOUNT trials showed average losses of 20–22% of body weight at the highest dose (15mg) over 72 weeks.

NUTRITION

Why Protein Intake is Critical on GLP-1 Medications

GLP-1 medications work by suppressing appetite significantly — sometimes too well. Many patients eat far less than they need, which sounds ideal for weight loss but creates a serious risk: muscle loss.

When you lose weight rapidly without adequate protein, your body breaks down muscle tissue for energy. Studies show that up to 40% of weight lost on GLP-1 medications can be muscle mass if protein intake is insufficient. This is called sarcopenia and can slow your metabolism long-term.

The general recommendation for people on GLP-1 medications is 1.2–1.6g of protein per kg of body weight per day, significantly higher than the standard 0.8g/kg recommended for sedentary adults. For a 80kg person, that means 96–128g of protein daily.

High-protein foods that are easy to eat even with reduced appetite include Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, eggs, chicken breast, fish, and protein shakes. The key is spreading intake across 3–4 small meals rather than trying to eat it all at once.

Use the protein calculator above to find your exact daily target based on your current weight and medication. Hitting your protein target consistently is one of the most important things you can do to preserve muscle while losing fat on GLP-1 therapy.

💡 Tip: Track your protein for the first 2 weeks on GLP-1 medication. Most patients are surprised by how little they're eating and how far below target they are.

Ozempic vs Wegovy vs Mounjaro vs Zepbound: Which GLP-1 Drug Wins?

The GLP-1 medication landscape has expanded rapidly. Here's a clear comparison of the four major options based on published clinical trial data:

Drug Active Ingredient Avg Weight Loss Approved For How Given
Ozempic Semaglutide 0.5–2mg 5–10% Type 2 Diabetes Weekly injection
Wegovy Semaglutide 2.4mg 15–17% Obesity / Weight Weekly injection
Mounjaro Tirzepatide 5–15mg 20–22% Type 2 Diabetes Weekly injection
Zepbound Tirzepatide 5–15mg 20–22% Obesity / Weight Weekly injection

Key difference: Mounjaro and Zepbound contain tirzepatide, which activates both GLP-1 and GIP receptors. This dual action makes it significantly more effective for weight loss than semaglutide-only drugs like Ozempic and Wegovy.

Cost and availability vary significantly by country. In the US, all four require a prescription. Ozempic and Mounjaro are technically for diabetes but are widely prescribed off-label for weight loss. Wegovy and Zepbound are the weight-management approved versions at higher doses.

GLP‑1 Protein FAQ

How much protein should I eat on GLP‑1?

Many people aim around 1.6–2.0g per kg of body weight, depending on health, activity, and medical guidance.

Can GLP‑1 medications cause muscle loss?

Weight loss may include some lean mass loss. Protein, strength training, sleep, and medical supervision can help reduce risk.
