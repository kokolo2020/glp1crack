GLP‑1 Protein FAQ

How much protein should I eat on GLP‑1?

Many people aim around 1.6–2.0g per kg of body weight, depending on health, activity, and medical guidance.

Can GLP‑1 medications cause muscle loss?

Weight loss may include some lean mass loss. Protein, strength training, sleep, and medical supervision can help reduce risk.

Which GLP‑1 medications does this support?

This tool is designed for general GLP‑1 nutrition planning, including Ozempic, Wegovy, Zepbound, Mounjaro, Rybelsus, and similar medications.

Is this medical advice?

No. This is an educational calculator. People using prescription medication should follow their doctor’s plan.
