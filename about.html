About This Site

A free, no-login tool to help people on GLP‑1 medication plan their protein and nutrition.

GLP‑1 medications like Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, and Zepbound reduce appetite — which is exactly how they help with weight loss, but it also makes it surprisingly easy to under-eat protein. Losing weight while protecting muscle takes deliberate planning, and most people just want a quick, clear number to aim for.

We built this calculator to give that number in seconds, then turn it into something practical: how much that is in real foods, how to spread it across meals, and what to watch for as you go. Everything is free and runs in your browser.

How our estimates are built

Our protein targets use widely cited ranges of roughly 1.6–2.0 grams per kilogram of body weight, adjusted for your goal. Calorie estimates use the Mifflin‑St Jeor equation for resting energy, multiplied by an activity factor and adjusted with a moderate deficit. These are standard, well-documented approaches — but they are general guides, not personalised medical advice.
